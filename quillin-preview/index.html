        Windshield Chip Repair

        Windshield Replacement

        Need a new windshield? Here at Quillin Auto Glass, we bring the replacement right to you. Taking care of your vehicle with the same attention we would give our own.

        ADAS Recalibration

        Newer vehicles watch the road through a camera behind the mirror for lane keeping and automatic braking. Change the glass it looks through and it has to be re-aimed, or it reads the road wrong.

        Door Glass Replacement

        A broken door window can leave your vehicle exposed and quickly become a stressful inconvenience. We come directly to you, carefully clean up the broken glass, install your new window, and make sure everything is working properly so you can get back to your day.

        Power Window Repair/Replacement

        Having trouble with a window that will not roll up or down? We can help! Our certified team will diagnose the issue to determine whether it needs a switch, motor, regulator, or something else. From there, we will recommend the right repair to get your window working properly again.

        Back Glass Replacement

        Let’s get your vehicle secure, protected, and back to normal without rearranging your day. We come directly to you, thoroughly clean out the broken glass, and carefully install your new back glass using professional-grade materials and proper installation techniques.

        Specialty Glass Replacement

        Our team is equipped to repair even the most unique parts: vent glass, quarter glass, mirrors, moldings, and everything in between. Not sure what part you need? Send us a photo, and we will take it from there!

        Common Questions

        The ones we get asked most.

        Rock chip repair

        Will the rock chip disappear?

        Our goal is to save your windshield and stop the damage spreading. It does look better afterwards, but it will not disappear completely. The resin we use dries clear, though not crystal clear like glass.

        Windshield replacement

        How long until I can drive?

        Thirty minutes to an hour and a half, depending on what you need. We can use a quick-set urethane if you are in a hurry, just ask.

        When can I remove the tape?

        After 24 hours.

        How long until I can wash my vehicle?

        Five days.

        What if it rains before the five days?

        Rain is fine. It is the pressure of a car wash we want to keep away from it, not water.

        Door glass

        What about the glass inside the door and the vehicle?

        Your door glass quote includes clearing the fragments out of the door and the visible glass inside the vehicle.
